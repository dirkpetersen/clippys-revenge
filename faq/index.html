FAQ¶

Does it work with the new Outlook?¶

No. It needs classic Outlook, because it drives Outlook through COM. The new Outlook has no COM interface.

Does it work with IMAP or POP accounts?¶

Not supported. An Exchange or Microsoft 365 mailbox is required.

Can it send email?¶

No. Clippy never sends anything, neither mail nor meetings, with or without AI. It has no send function at all, and the code is checked for this on every build. It saves drafts and you send them from Outlook. Meeting invites are saved unsent in your calendar too. See Meeting drafts and Security.

Why can't Clippy send a meeting invite?¶

Outlook's object model can't add a Teams or Zoom link, and you would add one by hand anyway. So Clippy saves the meeting unsent, marks it with the orange category Clippy draft invite, and lists it under Draft invites in the tray. You open it, add the link and send it. Clippy then removes its category.

What is the yellow number on the tray icon?¶

The attention badge. It counts meeting drafts waiting for you to add a link and send them. It disappears when you have sent them all.

Can it delete or move my mail?¶

No. It never deletes email, never empties Deleted Items and never moves your mail. There is one narrow exception, and it only concerns Clippy's own drafts: see the next question. Removing rows from Clippy's own search index is not touching mail.

Why did a draft go to Deleted Items?¶

Only an AI draft that Clippy wrote itself can go there. When a newer message arrives in the same thread, Clippy re-checks its earlier draft. If no reply is needed any more, or a better one was written, Clippy moves the old draft to Deleted Items (from where you can drag it back). It does this only when you haven't edited it, opened it or sent it, and the Activity window records it. Nothing is ever deleted for good. You can turn it off with Update or remove my AI drafts when a thread moves on in Settings, AI drafts. See Outdated drafts.

What does "Rescan emails from the last 24 hours" do?¶

It is in the tray, under AI drafts. It goes through the last day of mail, oldest first, drafts replies where one is needed and re-checks Clippy's own drafts in those threads. It asks first, showing how many Claude calls it may use, and stops at your daily limit. Click Cancel rescan to stop it. It works even when automatic drafting is off. See Rescan and the tray menu.

Why isn't my writing style used yet?¶

The first writing-style skill is built from your Sent Items. Clippy indexes Sent Items before the Inbox and builds the skill as soon as they are done, so you don't have to wait for the whole first backfill. Until then, drafts have no personal style. See Your writing style.

Which Claude model does it use?¶

By default the latest Opus: the model setting is opus, which always means the newest Opus. You can type sonnet or haiku the same way, or a full model name. See Settings.

Where can I see what Clippy did?¶

In the Activity window: double-click the tray icon. It lists syncs, tool calls, drafts and updates for the current session, without mail text.

Why do reply subjects start with "Re::"?¶

It is intentional. Reply drafts always start with Re:: (double colon).

Can it write replies for me?¶

Yes, if you turn on AI drafts. Clippy then asks Claude to draft replies to new mail in your style and saves them in Drafts. It never sends them. It is off by default and asks for your consent first.

Will the AI draft offer meeting times?¶

Yes, when an email asks for a meeting and Offer meeting times when an email asks for a meeting is on (the default). Clippy picks the times from your calendar itself, the model never sees them, and each option is its own line that you can delete. For senders in Europe it adds the time in CET. See Meeting times.

Why does a meeting time say CET in summer?¶

The label is always "CET" for Central European time, also while Europe is on summer time. The time itself uses the real offset (+02:00 in summer), so the number is right.

Why did a draft come out without a CET time for a sender in Europe?¶

Clippy guesses where the sender is from the Date header and the address ending, and can miss, for example for a .com address in winter UK time. See Senders in Europe. You can add or delete the CET part by hand.

An email got no draft and the Activity window says "low confidence". What now?¶

Claude wasn't sure enough that a reply was needed, below your Minimum confidence. Such an email is decided once and not retried. To get a draft anyway, select the email in Outlook and use Draft a reply to the selected email in the tray menu. See Emails Clippy decides not to draft.

Does AI drafting send my mail to Anthropic?¶

Yes, while it is on: the email being answered, thread excerpts, up to 5 related earlier emails, attachment names, the extracted text of attachments and of synced shared files (unless you turn that off), your facts file and your style guide go to Claude through your own Claude Code (or your API key). Once a week, with the writing-style update, samples of your sent mail go too, and up to 20 pairs of an AI draft and what you sent after editing it (unless you turn that off). Files themselves and images never do. See what is sent.

Does Clippy learn from how I edit its drafts?¶

Yes, since version 0.1.1, unless you turn it off. When you send a reply that began as an AI draft, Clippy notes what you changed. It keeps a redacted copy of the draft and of what you sent in its local index (at most 1,500 characters each, the newest 20 edited pairs, deleted after 30 to 90 days). Nothing leaves your PC then. At the next writing-style update (weekly, or Regenerate), if there are at least 3 pairs, up to 20 are sent to Claude, which adds an "Edits I usually make to drafts" section to your style. Drafts that used attachment text are never kept. Untick Learn from how I edit Clippy's drafts in Settings, AI drafts, to stop it. See Learning from how you edit drafts.

How do I make Clippy forget how I edited its drafts?¶

Turn off Learn from how I edit Clippy's drafts in Settings, AI drafts: nothing more is recorded or sent. That doesn't erase what is already stored; it ages out within 90 days. To erase it now, use Rebuild index… in the tray and choose My mailbox or All mailboxes (it deletes the index file that holds the records). Then press Regenerate in Settings to write a style without the "Edits I usually make to drafts" section.

Why does Activity say "noted how you edited 3 sent drafts"?¶

Clippy looked at your recently sent mail, found replies that started as its AI drafts and compared them with the drafts. It says how many it noted, including drafts you sent unchanged. See Activity.

Does Clippy read my attachments?¶

For AI drafts, yes, since v0.1.1 and unless you turn it off. It reads the text of PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, HTML and .eml attachments (and attached emails) of the email and its thread, and of OneDrive/SharePoint files linked in them that are synced to your PC, and sends that text to Claude for that one draft. It is never stored or indexed. Images are not read. See Attachments and shared files, and Settings to turn it off.

Why couldn't Clippy read my shared file?¶

When Clippy can't read a file from a link or cloud attachment, the draft starts with a yellow note, for example Clippy: Couldn't read the shared file Plan.docx (not synced to this PC). The reasons:

  • Not synced to this PC. Clippy never downloads from the network. It reads only the copy that your OneDrive sync client already keeps on this PC. The file must be in your OneDrive, a synced SharePoint or Teams library or folder, or a folder you added with Add shortcut to My files. Sync the library (in SharePoint, Sync) or add the shortcut, then rescan or draft the reply again. "Specific people" and other secret-token sharing links, Doc.aspx?sourcedoc=… links, and 1drv.ms and onedrive.live.com links can't be matched to a local file even when it is synced.
  • Not from your organization. The note says Clippy only opens shared files from people in your organization. Clippy opens a shared link only when the email came from you or from your own email domain (or a subdomain), so that outsiders can't make it put a file from your OneDrive into a draft. If your own address is at a free or consumer mail provider such as outlook.com, hotmail.com, gmail.com, yahoo.com, icloud.com or GMX, only links you sent yourself (or the shared mailbox's own address) are read. Links are taken only from the text each person wrote: links in quoted replies or forwarded mail are ignored. Attached files are read whoever sent them.
  • Not a OneDrive or SharePoint link. Dropbox, Google Drive and other links are never opened. There is no note for these.
  • The file is too slow. An online-only file is downloaded by OneDrive when Clippy opens it. If that takes longer than 10 seconds (or the draft's 20 seconds), the note says it took too long. While one PDF that took too long is still being processed, other PDFs are skipped with the same note until it finishes.
  • The file itself. password-protected, larger than 25 MB, no text in it, maybe a scan (Clippy has no OCR), the file seems damaged, or Clippy can't read .<ext> files for formats such as .doc, .xls, .rtf and .zip.
  • The setting is off. If Read attachments and synced shared files for drafts is unticked, no files are read and there are no notes.

Notes are shown only for files of the email itself and for earlier files the email mentions by name. More about the notes, with their exact texts, is in The yellow notes.

Where does Clippy keep the attachment text?¶

Nowhere. It is read into memory for one draft request and then dropped. The next draft in the same thread reads the file again. Outlook attachments pass through a private folder, %LOCALAPPDATA%\ClippysRevenge\ai-files\, for a moment and are deleted at once (and at start-up, if one was left). See Security.

What is the "Today: … tokens" line?¶

A tally of today's Claude calls and the tokens they used, shown while AI drafts consent exists: at the top of the tray's AI drafts menu and in the Activity window. For example Today: 18 AI calls, ~210k tokens (in 190k / out 20k). It counts drafts, re-checks, rescans and style updates, not calls that never started. Only counts are stored (30 days). See Token tally.

How do I stop drafts for one sender?¶

Select an email from them in Outlook and use Don't draft for this sender (or …this sender's domain…) in the tray's AI drafts menu. To undo, delete the line in Settings, AI drafts, Never draft for. See Don't draft for this sender.

Does Clippy work in a non-English Outlook?¶

Yes. Since v0.1.1 Clippy recognizes the Drafts and Deleted Items folders by their role, so they are skipped for related mail and earlier attachments whatever Outlook calls them. A folder indexed before v0.1.1 under its translated name keeps its old entries until you add the folder again or use Rebuild index…. See Drafts and Deleted Items in other languages.

To cut quoted history from mail, Clippy recognizes the reply headers Outlook writes in English, German, French, Spanish, Dutch, Italian, Swedish, Polish and Japanese (for example From:/Von:/Från:/差出人: blocks and "On … wrote:" lines). In other languages more of the quoted text may reach Claude as part of an email.

Can I tell the AI drafts my Zoom link or how I like to sign off?¶

Yes. Use Edit my facts… in the tray, under AI drafts. It opens a file with headings for your role, links, office hours, standard answers, people and How I want replies written. Whatever you write there is used in every draft, and your reply preferences win over the writing style. See Facts about you.

Does Claude see my older emails too?¶

A few. With Include related earlier emails on (the default), each draft request carries up to 5 earlier emails from the same mailbox that match the topic or the sender, including your own replies to that person, so the draft fits the history. You can turn it off in Settings, AI drafts. See Related earlier mail.

What is the yellow line at the top of an AI draft?¶

The reason line: one sentence starting Clippy: that says why the draft was written. Delete it before you send. Deleting it counts as editing the draft, so Clippy won't replace or move that draft later. You can turn the line off in Settings, AI drafts. See The reason line.

Does Clippy sign in to Claude or read my Claude login?¶

No. You sign in to Claude Code yourself in a terminal, and Clippy only asks it whether you are signed in. It never reads Claude Code's stored credentials.

Why didn't a mail get a draft?¶

It may have arrived before you turned AI drafts on, be older than the age limit, be a newsletter or automatic mail, match a never-draft rule, or not need a reply. See Rules.

Is the writing-style file safe?¶

It is a Claude Code skill in %USERPROFILE%\.claude\skills\my-email-style, so your interactive Claude Code sessions can load it. Review, edit or delete it any time. See the security note.

Does my mail go to the cloud?¶

Clippy has no cloud component. It runs on your PC and listens on 127.0.0.1. What Claude Code then sends to its model is the text of the mail it reads, so treat that as you would any use of an AI assistant with your mail. The optional AI drafts feature also sends mail to Claude, with your consent.

Do I need an app registration or admin consent?¶

No. There is no Graph API, Entra app registration or OAuth. If Outlook can open the mail, Clippy can use it.

Can Claude answer "who emails me the most?" or "how much mail do I get per month?"¶

Yes, in one call. Ask in plain language, for example "top 10 people who emailed me in the last year, with counts" or "mail per month since January". Claude uses email_stats, which counts mail in the local index by sender, sender domain, recipient, folder, month, week, weekday or hour. It returns counts and names, never mail text, and works while Outlook is closed.

Can Claude run its own searches with SQL?¶

Yes, with query_index: one read-only SELECT over three views of the index, including full-text search with snippets. Nothing can be changed and nothing outside those views can be read. See Security.

Does Outlook have to be running?¶

For indexing, yes. Clippy never starts Outlook on its own. Searching the existing index works while Outlook is closed. Tools that read live from Outlook (full messages not yet indexed, attachments, drafts, calendar) need it running.

Can Claude Code in WSL use it?¶

Yes, with WSL's mirrored networking. See Connect Claude Code.

Can other computers or web pages reach it?¶

No. It binds to 127.0.0.1 only, requires a token, and rejects any request that has an Origin header.

How far back does search go?¶

12 months by default. Change it in Settings, Index (1 to 120 months).

Are attachments searchable?¶

Attachment file names are searchable. Their contents are never indexed. To read an attachment, Claude saves it with get_attachment (or, from WSL, fetches it with attachment_link) and reads the file.

Where does it save attachments?¶

%USERPROFILE%\Downloads\Clippy's Revenge by default. Claude can only save to allowed folders, which you control in Settings, General.

Where do downloaded files go when Claude uses a download link?¶

Into the folder where Claude Code ran the curl command, for example your WSL project folder. With attachment_link, Clippy makes a one-time link and the shell downloads the file itself. Clippy keeps a private copy in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ClippysRevenge\links\ only until the download ends or 10 minutes pass, then deletes it. That folder is normally empty.

How do I get an attachment into WSL?¶

Ask Claude Code to fetch it. It uses attachment_link and runs the curl command it gets back. You need CLIPPY_TOKEN set in WSL once: see Set CLIPPY_TOKEN.

curl says 401 when Claude downloads an attachment¶

The shell doesn't have your token, or has an old one. Open Settings, Connection, press Copy WSL token line and put the line in ~/.bashrc in WSL, then source ~/.bashrc. After Regenerate auth token you must do this again. A 401 does not use up the link, so the same command works once the token is right (if the 10 minutes haven't passed).

curl says 404 when Claude downloads an attachment¶

The link is single use and lasts 10 minutes. A 404 means it was already used, has expired, was never made, or Clippy was restarted (which clears all links). Ask Claude for a new link. The link names the port it was made on, so after a port change old links no longer work either.

Can I download the same link twice?¶

No. Each link works once. Ask Claude for a new one.

Why does Claude say the attachment is too big for a link?¶

Links handle files up to 100 MB, with at most 10 open links, 200 MB open in total and 20 links per 10 minutes. For a bigger file, Claude uses get_attachment to save it on the Windows side. See the limits.

Can I use a shared mailbox?¶

Yes. Check it under tray, Shared mailboxes. See Shared mailboxes.

Does it start with Windows?¶

Yes by default. Turn it off with the Start with Windows item in the tray menu.

How do I update?¶

By default Clippy checks GitHub once a week and installs a newer version at its next start. To update at once, open About Clippy's Revenge… in the tray and press Update now, or run the install one-liner again. See Updates and Install.

How do I turn updates off?¶

Settings, General, Updates: choose Don't check (no automatic checks) or Only tell me (a balloon, no download). See Updates.

Does Clippy send anything to GitHub?¶

Only the weekly update check and the download, as plain requests that carry just the version number. No mail, no settings, no telemetry. See what is sent.

How do I uninstall?¶

See Uninstall.

Why does SmartScreen warn me?¶

The exe is not code-signed yet. The installer verifies its checksum.

Where are the logs?¶

%LOCALAPPDATA%\ClippysRevenge\logs. Use the tray's Open log folder.
