Tray menu¶

Clippy's Revenge has no main window. Everything lives in the paperclip icon in the system tray (you may need to click the ^ to see hidden icons). This page covers the icon, the right-click menu and the balloons. The other windows have their own pages: Settings and Activity.

Icon colors and tooltip¶

The icon shows what the app is doing. Hover over it for a short description. When several things happen at once, the highest row in this table wins.

Icon State Tooltip
Red Error Clippy — error: <short reason>
Grey with ! Outlook is not running, or not responding Clippy — Outlook is not running or Clippy — Outlook is not responding
Blue, animated First backfill (indexing mail) Clippy — indexing Inbox 1,240 / 8,000 (2025-11 → now)
Blue Pulling new mail Clippy — pulling new mail (Inbox +12)
Blue Removing mail older than the retention window Clippy — removing mail older than 12 months
Grey Indexing is paused Clippy — indexing paused
Blue AI drafts are being written Clippy — drafting replies (2 / 5)
Green, brief pulse Answering a tool call Clippy — search_emails (8 ms)
Green Idle Clippy — idle · 48,210 emails · synced 2 min ago

Tooltips are cut at 127 characters. Times read just now, N min ago, N h ago or N d ago. While a shared mailbox is syncing, the text names it, for example indexing helpdesk@… Inbox 300 / 2,000.

Double-click the icon to open the Activity window.

Attention badge¶

When something needs you, a small yellow badge with a number appears in the top-right corner of the icon, on top of whatever color the icon has. It shows 1 to 9, or 9+ for more. For now it counts your draft invites: meeting drafts that Claude saved and that you haven't sent yet.

The tooltip then ends with · 1 draft invite needs your attention (or · 3 draft invites need your attention). The badge goes away when the count reaches zero. While Outlook isn't running, it keeps the last count, because the meetings still wait for you.

Status lines¶

  • First line. The same text as the tooltip, but without "· synced …" while idle. For example Clippy — idle · 48,210 emails. While Outlook isn't running it reads Clippy — Outlook is not running.
  • Second line. Last sync: <time ago or never>, followed by · paused while paused, or · next in N min.

Both lines are greyed out and can't be clicked.

About Clippy's Revenge… Opens the About window: version, update status, Update now and links to the documentation and release notes. See Updates

If you start the app a second time, it opens Settings in the copy that is already running.

Shared mailboxes¶

Lists the Exchange mailboxes added to your Outlook profile, except your own, with a check mark on the ones Clippy indexes. See shared mailboxes.

  • Check a mailbox to start indexing it (its backfill starts at once).
  • Uncheck a mailbox and Clippy asks Stop indexing <name>? with Delete its index, Keep index file (the default) and Cancel. The mailbox is switched off either way, at once.
  • A mailbox you configured but that Outlook no longer lists shows (not in Outlook).
  • While Outlook isn't running, only configured mailboxes are listed, plus a greyed-out line Start Outlook to see more mailboxes.
  • Manage in Settings… opens the Index tab.

AI drafts¶

The submenu for AI drafts, top to bottom:

Item What it does When it is unavailable
Today: 18 AI calls, ~210k tokens (in 190k / out 20k) The first line, greyed out. Today's token tally: Claude calls since midnight and the tokens they used. It reads Today: no AI calls when there were none, ends in tokens unknown when Claude gave no numbers, and adds , <n> without token data when only some calls had numbers. Refreshed each time you open the menu Hidden until you have given AI drafts consent
Draft replies automatically Check mark. Turns automatic drafting on or off. The first time, Clippy shows the consent dialog. If Claude isn't ready (not signed in, no API key), it opens Settings on the AI drafts tab instead of switching on Greyed out while the email index isn't available, or while a toggle is in progress
Draft a reply to the selected email Drafts a reply to the one email you selected in Outlook, now, even when automatic drafting is off. Asks for consent first if you haven't given it. A balloon reports the result Greyed out while Outlook isn't running, while the index isn't available and while a rescan runs
Don't draft for this sender Adds the sender of the email selected in Outlook to Never draft for in Settings, with no dialog. Needs no consent. A balloon says I won't draft replies to <address> any more. You can undo this in Settings → AI drafts. Refuses your own address and an address that is already covered, and stops at 200 entries. Activity records AI drafts: added an entry to "Never draft for" (<n> entries). Existing drafts for that sender are left alone. To undo, delete the line in Settings, AI drafts, Never draft for. See Don't draft for this sender Greyed out while Outlook isn't running, while the index isn't available and while a rescan runs
Don't draft for this sender's domain… The same for the sender's whole domain (and its subdomains), after asking Never draft replies to anyone at <domain>? (Add or Cancel, the default). Refuses your own organization's domain (and its parent or subdomains). Undo it the same way: delete the domain line in Settings Same as above
Rescan emails from the last 24 hours… Goes through the last day of mail, oldest first, drafts replies where one is needed and re-checks Clippy's own drafts in those threads. Works with automatic drafting off. See below Greyed out while Outlook isn't running, while the index isn't available and while the rescan is starting
Show Drafts in Outlook Opens your Drafts folder in Outlook Hidden while Outlook isn't running. Greyed out while the index isn't available
AI drafts settings… Opens Settings on the AI drafts tab
Edit my facts… Opens your facts file (%USERPROFILE%\.claude\skills\my-email-context\SKILL.md) in Notepad. If the file is missing, Clippy creates the template first, even without AI drafts consent, because nothing is sent. If Notepad can't start, a balloon says Couldn't open the file: <reason> Greyed out while the AI drafts services aren't available

Rescan emails from the last 24 hours… works like this:

  1. It asks for consent if you haven't given it, and checks that Claude is ready. If not, a balloon says Claude isn't ready: <status>.
  2. It counts the emails it would look at. With none, a balloon says There are no emails from the last 24 hours to rescan. If today's limit is already used, a balloon says Today's AI draft limit (<cap>) is used up.
  3. It shows a confirmation, Rescan the last 24 hours?, with the number of emails, the number of Claude calls (one per email at most) and how many of today's calls are left. Choose Rescan or Cancel (the default).
  4. While it runs, the item reads Cancel rescan (d/t), for example Cancel rescan (3/12), and the tray tooltip reads Clippy — Rescanning the last 24 hours (3 / 12). Click it to stop. The rescan stops before the next email. An email whose answer has already arrived is finished first, so nothing is left half done.
  5. When it ends, a balloon shows the summary line (see Activity). Click the balloon to open Drafts if anything was drafted or updated.

A rescan stops by itself at the daily limit, when Claude's usage limit is reached, when Claude isn't signed in, when Outlook isn't running, or after 3 failed calls in a row. Nothing is ever sent. Details: Rescan emails from the last 24 hours.

Draft invites¶

Shown only while meeting drafts wait for you. The header carries the count, for example Draft invites (2). Below it, one line per meeting, sorted by start time:

Tue 10/15 10:00–11:00  Project sync
Wed 10/16 14:00–14:30  Budget review
  • Subjects are cut at 40 characters. A meeting in a shared mailbox's calendar shows the mailbox name in parentheses.
  • Only the first 15 meetings are listed. A last greyed-out line says and <n> more.
  • Click a line to open that meeting in Outlook. Add the Zoom or Teams link and send it there. Clippy then removes its category.
  • A meeting Clippy can't read the details of shows Draft invite saved <day> <date> <time>.
  • While Outlook isn't running or not responding, the lines are greyed out. While it isn't running, the last line says Start Outlook to open them.
  • If the meeting is gone from your calendar, a balloon says That draft invite is no longer in your calendar. If Outlook can't open it, the balloon says It looks like Outlook couldn't open that draft invite. Open it from your calendar.

Once, at the first AI draft that uses related mail, if you consented before this was added AI drafts now also send up to 5 related earlier emails from your mailbox to Claude, to write better replies. You can turn this off in Settings → AI drafts.
Once, at the first AI draft that reads attachments, if you consented before this was added AI drafts now also read attachments and shared files synced to this PC, and send their text to Claude, to write better replies. You can turn this off in Settings → AI drafts.

Balloon texts are cut at 255 characters. Balloons that report on their own are limited to one per minute. A balloon that hits the limit is dropped, not queued. The 2-minute balloons fire once per episode.
