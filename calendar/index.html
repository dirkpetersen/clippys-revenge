Calendar and meetings¶

Three tools work with your calendar: list_events, find_free_times and create_meeting_draft. They read Outlook live, so Outlook must be running. Clippy never sends anything: a meeting is saved unsent and you send it from Outlook.

Listing events¶

list_events returns events in a window, with recurring events expanded, at most 500, sorted by start. Without arguments it covers 7 days from today 00:00 (days can be 1 to 366, or Claude passes start and end). Each event has its id, subject, start, end, all-day flag, location, organizer, whether it recurs and its busy status. Pass mailbox to read a shared mailbox's calendar. It only reads; nothing is changed.

Finding a free time¶

find_free_times reads Outlook free/busy for you and the attendees, combines it with your working hours, and suggests the first free blocks that are long enough. Nothing is booked.

A typical flow:

  1. You paste a list of attendees, as copied from an Outlook address line.
  2. Claude calls find_free_times and shows suggestions such as 1. Tue 10/15 1-3 PT.
  3. You say "book number 2". Claude passes that suggestion's start and end to create_meeting_draft.

Things to know:

  • People whose free/busy can't be read, such as people outside your Exchange organization, are listed as unknown and ignored. Check with them yourself.
  • Working hours, business-days-only, the time-zone label and whether tentative counts as busy come from Settings, Scheduling. Claude can override the hours and business days per call.
  • Suggestions are whole free blocks, in local time. They are ranked: times right before or after your own meetings come first (within 15 minutes, on the same day), then times next to tentative meetings, then the rest, each group in date order. Free/busy can't tell a meeting from other busy time, so "next to a meeting" means "next to busy time". If your own calendar isn't checked (include_me off, or your free/busy can't be read), no time is ranked above another.
  • At most 50 attendees, and at most 60 days ahead.
  • The tool offers free time from now on, with no extra lead time. AI drafts add a lead time of their own; see AI drafts.
  • Besides the blocks, the result has offers of exactly the meeting length and an offer_text with one option per line, ready to paste into an email. For someone in Europe, Claude sets offer_cet and each line also shows the time in CET, with the options between 08:00 and 18:00 CET first and the others in a second list headed Outside European office hours:. See find_free_times.
  • Clippy's AI drafts use the same offers when an email asks for a meeting.

Meeting drafts¶

create_meeting_draft saves a meeting in your calendar without sending it. Clippy never sends anything, neither mail nor meetings. Outlook's object model can't add a Teams or Zoom link, so you add the link yourself and send the invite from Outlook.

The workflow:

  1. You ask Claude Code to book a time, for example "make the invite for option 2". Claude calls create_meeting_draft with the attendees, subject, start and end.
  2. The meeting is saved unsent in your calendar with the orange category Clippy draft invite.
  3. If the invite answers an email, Claude passes reply_to_id and Clippy also saves a note in Drafts.
  4. The tray icon shows a yellow badge with the number of draft invites, and the tray menu has Draft invites (n).
  5. You open the meeting from that list, or from your calendar. Add the Zoom or Teams link and click Send in Outlook.
  6. Within a few minutes Clippy sees that the invite has gone out and removes its category. Any other category you set stays, and the badge count goes down.

Before anything is created, every recipient must resolve in Outlook, and a reply_to_id must name an email Clippy can find. If either check fails, nothing is created and Claude is told which recipients or which id to fix.

Invite mode¶

Set it in Settings, Scheduling, Meeting invites:

Mode What happens
Draft only (default) create_meeting_draft saves the meeting unsent
Disabled The tool refuses. Claude is told to ask you to enable it

Older versions had a Send mode with a confirmation dialog. It is gone. A saved Send now loads as Draft only, and the confirmation timeout is ignored.

The category¶

Every meeting draft gets the category Clippy draft invite. If your category list doesn't have it yet, Clippy adds it once, in orange. It never changes a category of that name that you already have, and never removes it from your list.

When you have sent the meeting, Clippy removes only this category and saves the meeting. Saving a category change doesn't send anything. If you removed the category yourself, Clippy changes nothing. It skips a meeting while you have it open in Outlook and tries again later.

Draft invites in the tray¶

Draft invites (n) is in the tray menu while at least one meeting draft is waiting, and hidden when there is none. Each line shows the day, time and subject, for example Tue 11/18 10:00–11:00 Project kickoff. Meetings in a shared mailbox's calendar add the mailbox name. The list is sorted by start and shows at most 15 lines, then a greyed and <n> more. Click a line to open the meeting in Outlook. Clicking only opens it; it writes nothing. After you restart Clippy, lines may read Draft invite saved <day and time> until the first check.

Clippy checks the list when you open the menu and after sync cycles, at most every five minutes. A meeting you deleted is dropped from the list. A meeting that ended more than 30 days ago and was never sent is dropped too, but it keeps its category. If a meeting has gone from your calendar when you click it, a balloon says That draft invite is no longer in your calendar. and the line is removed.

While Outlook isn't running, the lines are greyed out and the last line says Start Outlook to open them. While Outlook is not responding they are greyed out without that line.

The badge on the tray icon¶

While draft invites are waiting, a small yellow badge with their number sits in the top-right corner of the tray icon: 1 to 9, then 9+. The tooltip adds · 2 draft invites need your attention. The badge keeps its last count while Outlook is closed, because the meetings still need you, and disappears when the count reaches zero. See the badge on the tray page.

The note in Drafts¶

When Claude passes reply_to_id (the id of the email that asked for the meeting), Clippy saves a reply draft to that email. The reply starts with a highlighted note:

[[Draft invite saved in your calendar: Tue 10/15 10:00–11:00 PT "Project sync". Open it from the Clippy tray (Draft invites) or your calendar, add the Teams or Zoom link and send it. Delete this note before you send this reply.]]

Below the note is an empty paragraph for your own text. Delete the note before you send the reply. If an AI draft already answers the email, it is left alone, and the note is a second reply draft.

For a meeting in a shared mailbox's calendar the note says `in the calendar of

` and `or that calendar` instead. If the note can't be saved, the meeting is still saved and the result has a `note_error` that says why, for example `The meeting was saved, but the note in Drafts couldn't be created: .` ### Shared mailboxes With `mailbox`, the meeting is saved in that shared mailbox's calendar, and free/busy checks include that mailbox instead of yours. You need delegate or editor rights on that calendar; without them nothing is created and Claude is told to ask you to get access or to leave `mailbox` empty. The category is set on the meeting, but Outlook shows it without the orange color unless that mailbox's category list has it.
